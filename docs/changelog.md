# Changelog

Every merged PR adds a line here, newest first, under `Unreleased`. Keep each line short: what
changed and the PR number. When a TestFlight or App Store build ships, move the `Unreleased`
lines under a version heading.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · Area · What changed (#PR)`

## Unreleased

- 2026-11-06 · Data · Species roster grown from 361 to 1,090 (55 extinct), across 33 classes, 175 orders and 474 families. New `gen_taxonomy.py --screen` checks candidate titles against the bar for new species (D-019, proposed): Wikidata species or subspecies, 12,000+ byte article, free lead image, 3+ sharp photos under a Python mirror of the app's photo rules (D-018). No existing ids changed. Fixed the bird tier example in product.md for the new group sizes.

- 2026-11-06 · App · Achievements (D-016): new Awards tab with tiered goals for species met, for every species, each IUCN status and each phylum, class and order with 3+ bundled species. Targets 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, then every 50, scaled to each group's size; SF Symbol rosette medals; a banner when a tier is reached and a tab badge for unseen tiers. New pure `AchievementTiers` and `AchievementBoard`, `AchievementsStore` (`achievements.v1`), `TaxonomyIndex.taxa(rank:)` and `allEntries`, with tests.
- 2026-11-06 · Content · Better photos: each species' photos are now ranked best first (new pure `PhotoQuality`): Commons Featured, Quality and Valued images, big originals, portrait and near-square frames that fill the screen, close-ups ("heads", "portrait", "looking at viewer"), and photos that name the species rank higher; habitat shots, specimens and skulls, white-background cut-outs and Commons problem categories rank lower. Soft originals (under 1200 × 800) are dropped when a species has 2+ sharp photos. Fixed: sizes now use the original file, because Commons reports the requested thumbnail width for small originals, which let 600 px files through. Junk filter also reads Commons categories (maps, banknotes, cladograms, icons...) and matches short words whole ("Orange-bellied parrot" and "photograph" were being dropped). Artwork also detected from categories (woodcuts, illustrations...). Photos load at 1920 px, or Wikimedia's 3840 px/original rendition when a full-screen crop would otherwise be upscaled (Wikimedia now only serves standard thumbnail widths). Content cache moves to `species-content.v2` so everyone gets the new ranking.
- 2026-11-06 · Process · Architecture review gate (D-014) suspended at Maxwell's request: no design approval or architect sign-off; PR authors merge once CI is green. Architecture rules still apply.
- 2026-11-06 · App · Maxwell's feedback round: the intro card is now a short fact sheet (Lives in, Eats, size, Weight) from Wikidata, with one verbatim Wikipedia sentence (≤ 90 characters) where Wikidata has nothing (D-017; new `SpeciesProfile`, `WikidataClient`, `SpeciesProfileBuilder`, `FactSheet`; content cache schema v3). Species sets are at most intro + 4 cards and never show an image twice. Fact cards highlight the number and the word after it inside the sentence instead of a stand-alone big number. (#20)
- 2026-11-06 · App · Onboarding, the species detail sheet and the Met grid now load through the injected content provider and image loader (no more `WikipediaContentProvider.shared` or `AsyncImage`); Met tiles decode at tile size. Onboarding gets a photo credit on the welcome screen and a "Photo credits" list for the tiles. Met entries drop the transitional `imageURL`. New pure `HeroImage` (one hero rule outside the feed) and `SpeciesThumbnail` component (W3 PR B).
